Case study · Custom business system

The ERP, POS and CRM that runs my jewellery brand.

Shopify handled the storefront and nothing else, so I built one system for billing, GST invoices, inventory, customers, reports and Shopify import/export. It isn't a portfolio exercise. I use it every day to run Swasole.

The problem

Orders in three places

Shopify, shop-counter and wholesale sales had no single view of revenue or stock.

Bills by hand

GST invoices were made manually and were slow to correct.

Hidden costs

No per-product landing cost or margin, so profit was a guess.

Inside the system

Real app, made-up data. Cost, margin and wholesale rates are blurred.

Imports, security and tech

Shopify in and out

Import products, customers and orders from CSV, matched by SKU or name. Export new products back.

Excel bill import

Upload old shop bills from a spreadsheet; lines are grouped into invoices.

Login and roles

Supabase Auth with Owner, Admin, Staff and View-only roles, plus row-level security.

Backup and restore

One-click JSON backup so a lost database isn't a lost business.
