GST billing and a wholesale portal.

The two parts clients ask about most: invoices that reconcile to the penny, and a private ordering link for each retailer.

GST-inclusive billing

Customers pay the shelf price. The invoice backs the GST out of it so the total never changes.

  • Taxable value = total ÷ (1 + GST rate)
  • Discount on the subtotal, GST after it
  • CGST + SGST in-state, IGST between states
  • HSN and MRP on every line, plus amount in words
  • Bank details and terms printed on the bill
